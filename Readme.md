# Eldritch Aquarium

**An Uncanny Mercantile incremental economy sim**  
Built in Godot 4.x — [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/Darkgecko777/EldritchAquarium)

## Overview
Run a shady home aquarium research operation for a mysterious interstellar pet supply company. Order suspicious shipments through vintage comic ads, crack them open, feed eldritch pets, manage a living tank ecosystem, and push your specimens to their breaking point for permanent upgrades.

**Core Fantasy**: Cute aquatic pets mutate into tentacled horrors. Every run is a fresh experiment that ends in glorious, inevitable death — fueling your shady corporate science empire through prestige resets.

**Target**: Short-to-medium sessions with meaningful active tending, strategic depth, and high replayability via distinct starter builds.

## Core Loop
1. Click the comic ad → Order themed shipment (matched to your current starter pet).
2. Food/organs drop into the tank — mixed sizes with controllable RNG.
3. **Active Tending**: Pets auto-consume preferred sizes; **click released resource globs** (Insight/Biomatter) for bonuses. Mismatches rot → Pollution.
4. Evolve your starter through 5 stages (10 total choices) via consumption.
5. Pollution builds and dampens gains → comics warn you → pets eventually die (Pollution threshold or Overburden).
6. Spend **Forgotten Mnemonic Fragments** in the main prestige tree to improve shipments, evolution speed, Pollution tolerance, unlock new pets, etc.
7. Reset → Fresh starter RNG + new evolution paths. Scale higher.

## Key Features
- **3 Starter Playstyles** (one per run, RNG after unlocks):
  - **Freaky Goldfish** – Aggressive medium-food pusher.
  - **Abyssal Minnows** – Chaotic small-bit swarm.
  - **Remora Horror** – Stable large-food Pollution converter (unique Overburden death).
- **9 Total Pets** across Starters / Boosters / Interactors.
- **5-Stage Evolutions** (2 choices per stage) — reset on prestige.
- **Active + Idle Balance**: Click collection early, prestige unlocks more automation.
- **Comic-Driven Narrative**: 4-panel scientist banter at thresholds — escalating madness and humor.
- **No Lose States**: Suboptimal play just shortens runs and accelerates Fragments.

## Resources
- **Eldritch Insight** – Shipments & actions.
- **Abyssal Biomatter** – Growth & evolutions.
- **Forgotten Mnemonic Fragments** – Prestige spending.
- **Pollution** – Global dampener that pressures resets.
