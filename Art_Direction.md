# Art Direction - Eldritch Aquarium
**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: 2026-06-04  
**Status**: Living Document (Primary reference for Grok Imagine asset creation)

## Core Aesthetic
**Style**: Retro comic book advertisement meets Sea Monkeys nostalgia, infused with playful eldritch horror.  
**Influences**: Silver Age comics, vintage 1960s–70s mail-order catalogs, *Cuphead* (for bold stylization), *Spore* creature weirdness, and classic Sea Monkeys ads.

**Key Visual Traits**:
- Bold, clean black ink outlines
- Halftone shading and dot patterns for texture
- Vibrant but slightly "off" color palette (deep teals, purples, neon pinks/greens, muted yellowed paper tones)
- Dynamic impact lines, starbursts, and motion accents
- Cartoonish proportions that start cute and become increasingly grotesque/humorous as pets evolve
- Slight paper texture or comic panel borders on UI elements

**Tone Progression**:
- Early game: Whimsical, approachable, nostalgic fun
- Mid game: Noticeably weird and uncanny
- Late game: Gloriously grotesque but still playful and comic-book flavored

## UI Style (Fully Integrated)
- All interfaces styled as pages from a shady "ACME Void Supply Co." comic book catalog.
- Shop panel: Thick comic borders, "ORDER NOW!" starbursts, coupon-style buttons, fake guarantee seals.
- Intro sequence: Presented as a full-page vintage comic book advertisement.
- Resource counters: Printed stamp / ledger style.
- Pollution meter: "Water Quality Warning" comic panel that degrades visually.
- Madness events: Speech bubbles, thought balloons, and fourth-wall breaking comic gags.
- Notifications: "KRAK!", "SPLORCH!", "MUNCH!" impact text with classic comic sound effects.

## Asset Categories & Priorities

### 1. Core Game Objects
- Shipping containers (closed, landed, opening states) — retro sci-fi comic style with hazard labels
- Organs (basic set + variations) — gooey, wobbly, cartoonish with tiny eyes/mouths for personality
- Pets (multi-stage evolution lines) — starting with adorable comic Sea Monkey larvae

### 2. Tank & Environment
- Base tank background (layered water, bubbles, faint comic panel lines)
- Prestige cosmetic layers (sunken comic castles, deep-sea diver figures, eldritch coral structures, sunken ships, etc.)

### 3. VFX & Feedback
- Consumption effects, growth bursts, evolution flashes
- Pollution particles and water tinting
- Comic impact lines and text pop-ups

### 4. UI Elements
- Button styles, panel frames, catalog fold-outs
- Speech bubbles and madness overlays

## Generation Guidelines (For Grok Imagine)
- Always specify: "bold black ink outlines, halftone shading, retro comic book style, Sea Monkeys influence"
- Include "game asset sprite sheet ready" or "isolated on transparent background" when needed
- Request multiple angles/states where relevant (idle, eating, evolved)
- Maintain consistency across evolution stages
